      Ring size guide

      Measure the finger you will wear the ring on, at the end of the day when fingers are a little bigger. Smart rings are thicker than most rings, so a snug fit reads best.

      Three ways to find your size

      1. Print the ring sizer and wrap the paper strip around your finger.
      2. Lay a ring you already wear on the printed circles and find the one that matches the inside edge.
      3. Wrap a strip of paper around your finger, mark where it meets, and measure the length in millimetres.
