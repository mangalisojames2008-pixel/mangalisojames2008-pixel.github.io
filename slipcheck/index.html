06

How it works

Four steps, from the price on your slip to the chance it lands.

2.003.403.80
Odds

We take the bookmaker's prices and remove the margin they build in.

1.56–1.10
Expected goals

From those fair prices we work out how many goals each team should score. Team form can be blended in.

Score grid

Expected goals give the chance of every score, from 0-0 up to 10-10.

47%
Your chance

Each pick wins on some scores. We add those up. Picks on the same match are worked out together; different matches are multiplied.

It's an estimate, not a forecast. It doesn't know about injuries, line-ups or weather unless you add them through the form numbers. Because of the bookmaker's margin, most bets lose money over time.
