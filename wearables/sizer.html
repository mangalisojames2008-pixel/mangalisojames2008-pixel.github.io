Printable ring sizer

For the Colmi R10 and R12, sizes 7 to 13. Print on A4 at actual size, then use the strip or the circles.

  1. In the print window, set the scale to 100% or Actual size, not "Fit to page".
  2. Measure the 50 mm check line with a ruler. If it is not 50 mm, the sizes will be wrong. Print again.
  3. Measure at the end of the day, on the finger you will wear the ring on.

On a phone with no printer? Wrap a strip of paper around your finger, mark where it meets, and measure it with a ruler. The table below gives the size.

NEXERA ring sizer, sizes 7 to 13. Print at 100%.

Check line: must measure 50 mm

1. Paper strip

Cut out the strip. Wrap it around your finger, numbers facing out, so it is snug but slides over the knuckle. The number where the start of the strip meets is your size.

2. A ring you already wear

Lay the ring on the circles. Your size is the circle that lines up with the inside edge of the ring. Between two? Pick the bigger one.
