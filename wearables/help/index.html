How long does it take?

Orders paid before 12:00 on a working day are packed the same day. Delivery after dispatch: main centres: usually 2–3 working days; outlying areas 3–5.

Some products are marked "Ships from supplier" on their product page. Those take 10–20 working days, and we tell you this before you pay.

How do I track my order?

You get a tracking number by email or WhatsApp as soon as your parcel is handed to the courier. If it hasn't moved for 3 working days, message us and we'll chase it.

What if I'm not home?

The courier contacts you to arrange a new time. To avoid missed deliveries, choose a locker, or a work address where someone is always in.

My parcel arrived damaged or something is missing

Take a photo of the box and the item, and message us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll replace the item or refund you. You don't pay for anything that went wrong in transit.

Do you deliver outside South Africa?

Not yet. We deliver to South African addresses and lockers only.

02 · Returns & refunds

Changed your mind? You have 10 days.

  1. Tell usWhatsApp or email us within 10 days of delivery with your order number. No reason needed.
  2. Send it backWe send you the return address or a locker code. Pack it with everything it came with.
  3. Get your moneyWe refund you within 30 days of cancelling, usually much sooner, to the way you paid.
Who pays for return delivery?

Change of mind: you pay the cost of sending it back, as the law allows. Faulty, damaged or wrong item: we pay, and we arrange collection.

What condition does it need to be in?

For a change-of-mind return, the item should come back with all its parts, charger, straps and box. Trying a watch or ring on is fine, but it shouldn't be worn for days, scratched or damaged. If it comes back damaged, we may deduct the reasonable cost of the damage, and we'll explain it.

Can I swap for a different size or colour?

Yes. Message us within 10 days. Ring size swaps are free if we have the size in stock and the ring is returned unworn and undamaged.

Your rights in law

Online purchases come with a cooling-off right under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act: you can cancel within 7 days of receiving the goods, without a reason or penalty. Our 10-day window gives you more than that. Faulty goods are covered separately by the Consumer Protection Act (see Warranty). Nothing in our policies takes away your rights under South African law.

03 · Warranty

6-month warranty on everything

If a product is faulty, stops working properly, or isn't of the quality you'd reasonably expect within 6 months of delivery, you choose whether we repair it, replace it, or refund you. We pay to collect it and send it back.

How to claim
  1. Message us with your order number and a short video or photo of the problem.
  2. We may ask you to try one fix first (e.g. reset and re-pair the device). That's to save you time, not to delay your claim.
  3. We collect it, then repair, replace or refund, whichever you chose.
What's not covered

Damage from accidents, drops, cracked screens, tampering, or use against the instructions (e.g. a watch rated for splashes used for diving). Normal wear such as scratches on the case or strap.

After a repair

If we repair something and the same fault, or a new one, appears within 3 months of the repair, we'll replace it or refund you.

Batteries

Batteries are covered for the full warranty. Battery life gets shorter with use over time, which is normal. A battery that won't hold a charge or swells is a fault.

This follows section 56 of the Consumer Protection Act, which gives you a six-month implied warranty of quality and lets you choose the remedy.

04 · Ring size

Find your ring size

Smart rings fit more snugly than jewellery because of the sensors inside. A ring that's too loose spins and gives poor readings. Pick the finger you'll wear it on (most people choose the index finger) and measure at the end of the day, when fingers are slightly bigger.

1 · Measure with string or paper

Wrap a thin strip of paper around the base of your finger, mark where it overlaps, then measure the length in millimetres.

Your size will show here.

2 · Match a ring you already own

First calibrate: hold a bank card to the screen and drag until the box matches its width. Then lay your ring on the circle and resize the circle to the inside edge.
