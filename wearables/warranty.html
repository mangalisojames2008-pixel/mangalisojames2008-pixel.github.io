Warranty

If a watch, ring or case stops working the way it should, we sort it out.

What is covered

Every product has the 6-month warranty the Consumer Protection Act gives on goods that are faulty, unsafe or do not work as described. Within 6 months of delivery you choose a repair, a replacement or a refund.

Watches and smart rings: [MAKER'S WARRANTY, e.g. 12 months, to be confirmed with the supplier].

What is not covered

  • Cracked screens, dents and scratches from drops or knocks.
  • Water damage beyond the product's rating. Check each product page: some are fine for swimming, the Colmi R12 is not.
  • Normal wear on straps, buckles and case linings.
  • Products opened, repaired or changed by someone else.

How to claim

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your name, what you bought and when.
  2. Send a photo or short video that shows the problem. Many app and pairing problems are fixed in a few minutes this way.
  3. If it needs to come back, we tell you how to send it. Once we have it, we repair, replace or refund within [10 working days].

Battery life

Battery figures on product pages are the maker's figures for typical use. Always-on screens, GPS, calls and frequent readings use more power, so real life is often shorter. A battery that drops far below normal use in the first 6 months counts as a fault.

Health readings

Heart rate, SpO2, sleep and stress readings are for fitness and general wellness. They are not medical devices and are not covered for medical accuracy.
