    Questions

    Before you order

    How do I pay?

    Tap Checkout in your cart. The payment options, the delivery cost and the total are shown before you pay.

    How long does delivery take?

    Delivery takes 10–20 business days. Most of our products come from our suppliers overseas, so it takes longer than from a local shop. The law gives us 30 days to deliver, unless we agree a longer time with you. If we can't make it, we tell you, and you can cancel for a full refund. See delivery and returns.

    Will it work with my phone?

    The watches and rings pair with Android phones and iPhones through each maker's free app. Send us your phone model and we check before you order.

    Which ring size do I need?

    Smart rings come in sizes 7 to 13. Use the printable ring sizer, or measure a ring you already wear. If you are between sizes, ask us first.

    How does a band with no screen work?

    The Spovan H7 has no display. It sits on your wrist and the app on your phone shows your sleep, heart rate and heart-rate variability. It is rated IP68 for dust and water. See the H7.

    Are these medical devices?

    No. Heart rate, SpO2, sleep and stress readings are for fitness and general wellness. They do not diagnose or treat anything. Speak to a doctor or clinic about any health concern.

    What if something is faulty?

    Faulty products can come back within 6 months for a repair, replacement or refund. See the warranty page.

    Tell us what you train for and your budget. We reply with a straight answer.
