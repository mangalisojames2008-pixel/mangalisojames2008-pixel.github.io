Changed your mind

Online orders have a 7-day cooling-off period under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. Tell us on WhatsApp or by email within 7 days of delivery, send the product back unused in its original packaging, and we refund what you paid for it. The cost of sending it back is yours.

Wrong ring size

Tell us within 7 days of delivery and send the ring back unworn, with its charger and box. We swap it for another size if we have it in stock, or refund you as above. Check the ring sizer before you order to avoid the wait.

Faulty or damaged on arrival

Send us a photo or short video on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. We arrange the collection and replace or refund it. Faults that show up later are covered by the warranty.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back by EFT to the account you name, within 30 days of us receiving the product, as the law requires. Most are paid within [7 working days].
