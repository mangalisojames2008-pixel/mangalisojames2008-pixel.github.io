    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    How do I place an order?

    Add products to your bag, open the bag and tap Send on WhatsApp or Send by email. Your list is written for you, so you only need to press send.

    Why does a product say "Price on request"?

    The price is confirmed when you send your order, so the reply you get is for your exact model, colour and storage.

    Can I order more than one device at once?

    Yes. Add everything you want to your bag and change the quantity there. It all goes in one message.

    Is NEXERA part of Apple?

    No. NEXERA is an independent store that sells Apple products. Apple, iPhone, AirPods, MacBook and Apple Watch are trademarks of Apple Inc.
